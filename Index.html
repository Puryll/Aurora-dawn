Rules & expectations

Peace, respect, and good vibes for everyone.

Discord Rules

  • Respect everyone and avoid harassment, bullying, discrimination, hate speech, or slurs.
  • Keep the server clean: no illegal substance talk, NSFW content, gore, offensive material, or doxxing.
  • No spamming, repeated pings, or flooding chats in Discord or in-game.
  • No advertising without staff approval, including DMs and in-game promotion.
  • Voice chat should be respectful and quiet; normal rules still apply in VC.
  • No e-dating, weird flirting, or inappropriate behavior.
  • One account only; if you need an alt, create a ticket with a valid reason.
  • Use English in main channels; use the international channel if needed.

Minecraft Rules

  • No griefing, stealing, or borrowing items without permission.
  • No unwanted PvP; duels are allowed only when both players agree.
  • No hacks, cheats, x-ray, fly hacks, kill aura, or other unfair advantages.
  • No exploit abuse, bug abuse, dupes, or unintended mechanics.
  • No impersonation or mini-modding.
  • One Minecraft account per player, and no account switching mid-season.
  • Keep public areas clean and avoid messy clutter or pointless builds.
  • No begging or DMing staff/players for favors, items, or ability changes.
  • Allowed client mods include Freecam, BridgingMod, Fullbright, Litematica, ReplanterPlus, Xaero's MiniMap, and World Map.
  • Username and team names must stay appropriate.

Age Requirement

  • Players must be 14–35 to join and apply.
  • Applicants under 14 or over 35 will be denied.
  • Lying about age may result in punishment.

Punishments

  • Warning — a maximum of 7 warnings before a permanent ban.
  • Mute
  • Temporary Ban
  • Permanent Ban

Staff may skip warning stages for severe offenses. Aurora Dawn is a peaceful survival community, so respect, maturity, and common sense are expected from everyone.
